About me

I'm a graduate student in Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University, where I work on reinforcement learning and long-horizon robot learning with Prof. Max Simchowitz.

Previously, I was an ML researcher at MBZUAI, where I worked on decentralized LLM fine-tuning under Prof. Praneeth Vepakomma. Before that, I was a Quantitative Trader at Optiver in Amsterdam. I completed my undergraduate studies at IIT Bombay, majoring in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Machine Learning. There, I worked on multi-armed bandits with Prof. Nikhil Karamchandani and Prof. Jayakrishnan Nair.

My research interests lie in reinforcement learning, robot learning, and sequential decision making.
